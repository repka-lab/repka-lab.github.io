Four AI models call Bitcoin every week. Every call is logged before anyone sees it.

On Monday, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok each answer one question: does BTC end the week higher or lower than it started? The call goes into a public ledger first; only then does a video or post about it go out. On Friday it is checked, and misses stay on the record next to the hits.

Who is Repka?

Repka is the grandpa who hosts the weekly videos: an animated AI character, not a real person.
