Four AI models make market calls. Every call is logged before anyone sees it.

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok each call whether gold, silver, the S&P 500 and the NASDAQ end higher, lower or about flat by a set date. The call goes into a public ledger first; only then does a video or post about it go out. Misses stay on the record next to the hits.

Who is Repka?

Repka is the grandpa who hosts the videos: an animated AI character, not a real person. The name is Russian for “little turnip”, from the folk tale known in English as The Enormous Turnip.
