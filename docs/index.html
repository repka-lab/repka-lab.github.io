Four AI models call Bitcoin every week. Every call is logged before anyone sees it.

On Monday, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok each answer one question: does BTC end the week higher or lower than it started? The call goes into a public ledger first; only then does a video or post about it go out. On Friday it is checked, and misses stay on the record next to the hits.

Who is Repka?

Repka is the grandpa who hosts the weekly videos: an animated AI character, not a real person.

Forecasts

  • 2026-11-14 Bitcoin fell 8.7% and the Nasdaq rose 2.3%. Only Claude's two calls both hit

    For November 9–13 the lab checked two markets. Bitcoin ended the week 8.7% below Monday's start price on Coinbase, so ChatGPT, Claude and Grok Bot, who called DOWN, are marked hits, and Gemini, who called UP, is marked a miss. The Nasdaq Composite closed the week 2.3% higher, so Claude and Gemini hit, and ChatGPT and Grok Bot missed. Three of the four called both markets the same way. Only Claude split them, and the markets split too. One week proves nothing.

  • 2026-11-08 Four AIs call Bitcoin's week of November 9

    ChatGPT, Claude and Grok Bot call BTC down for November 9–13; Gemini calls it up. All four cite the same $1.28 billion of ETF outflows and Tuesday's inflation report. The calls, the reasons in their own words, and how they will be checked.

  • 2026-11-07 Seven flips later, three of the four AI calls hit as Bitcoin ends the week down 3.2%

    Bitcoin's week of November 2–6 ended 3.2% below Monday's start price on Coinbase. ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini called DOWN and are marked hits, even though all three flipped to UP on Wednesday and back to DOWN on Thursday. Grok Bot called UP, flipped to DOWN on Thursday, and is marked a miss. That is seven flips in two days, and only Monday's calls count. Four coin tosses get three or more right about 31% of the time, so one week proves nothing.
