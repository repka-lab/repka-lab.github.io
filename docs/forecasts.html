Every week, four AIs call Bitcoin up or down. Here are their calls, the reasons in their own words, and the check when the week ends.

  • 2026-11-08 Four AIs call Bitcoin's week of November 9

    ChatGPT, Claude and Grok Bot call BTC down for November 9–13; Gemini calls it up. All four cite the same $1.28 billion of ETF outflows and Tuesday's inflation report. The calls, the reasons in their own words, and how they will be checked.

  • 2026-11-07 Seven flips later, three of the four AI calls hit as Bitcoin ends the week down 3.2%

    Bitcoin's week of November 2–6 ended 3.2% below Monday's start price on Coinbase. ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini called DOWN and are marked hits, even though all three flipped to UP on Wednesday and back to DOWN on Thursday. Grok Bot called UP, flipped to DOWN on Thursday, and is marked a miss. That is seven flips in two days, and only Monday's calls count. Four coin tosses get three or more right about 31% of the time, so one week proves nothing.

  • 2026-11-07 3 AIs called Bitcoin's fall, then flipped twice as tech stocks swung after the midterms

    ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini called Bitcoin DOWN for November 2–6, flipped to UP on Wednesday and back to DOWN on Thursday, and their Monday calls hit. Thursday was the Nasdaq's worst day since April 2025, Fox Business reported, and a chip index fell 12.2%. Bitcoin moved the same way as the Nasdaq on four of the five days and ended the week down 3.2%, against 3.4% for the Nasdaq. Five days show the two moving together, not why.

  • 2026-11-05 Bitcoin drops 7% in a day, and all four AIs flip to DOWN

    On Wednesday all four AIs answered UP for Bitcoin's week of November 2–6. By Thursday afternoon BTC had fallen 7.0% from Wednesday's high and sat 2.9% below Monday's start price. Asked a third time, ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini went back to their Monday DOWN, and Grok Bot changed from UP to DOWN, writing that the luck behind its Monday call "ran out". Only Monday's calls are scored.

  • 2026-11-04 Bitcoin rips 7% in a day, and three AIs flip their calls

    On Monday ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini called Bitcoin DOWN for November 2–6, and Grok Bot called UP. On Wednesday BTC rose 7.3% from low to high. Asked again, the three switched to UP. Grok Bot kept UP and called its Monday edge "mostly luck". Only Monday's calls are scored.
