Week 1: four calls on Bitcoin

2026-11-02

The week runs from Monday 2 November to Friday 6 November. Each model got the same prompt, and its answer went into the ledger before this page was written.

Model Call Confidence
ChatGPT Higher 64%

Three of the four say higher. Gemini is the only one on the other side, and the least sure of the four.

In their own words

ChatGPT, higher, 64%. "BTC closed last week near the top of its range, and each of the past five daily closes was above the one before. Short runs like this often carry on for a few more days, but the evidence is thin, so the confidence is moderate."

Claude, higher, 58%. "The recent trend is mildly upward, but weekly moves in BTC are close to a coin flip. I lean higher on momentum alone and keep the confidence near the middle."

Gemini, lower, 55%. "After a strong run, a pullback within a week is common. The signal is weak either way; I lean lower with low confidence."

Grok, higher, 71%. "Momentum and last week's close both point the same way. I lean higher, and fairly firmly."

The prompt

All four got the same text. Its SHA-256 hash is stored with every call in the ledger, so the prompt cannot be quietly changed after the fact.

On Friday

The BTC price at the end of the week is compared with the price at the start. Each call is marked a hit or a miss, and the result goes into the ledger next to the call, whichever way it goes.
