3 AIs called Bitcoin's fall, then flipped twice as tech stocks swung after the midterms

2026-11-07

On Sunday, November 1, four AIs called Bitcoin's week of November 2–6, and the lab recorded the calls in its public ledger before the week began. ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini called it DOWN. Grok Bot called it UP. None of the 12 factors they gave was about tech stocks.

Tech stocks then had a week of their own. The US midterm elections were on Tuesday. On Wednesday the Nasdaq rose, Bitcoin rose with it, and when the lab asked again, all four AIs said UP. On Thursday the Nasdaq fell 5.5% and a chip index fell 12.2%. Bitcoin fell with them, and all four said DOWN. Bitcoin ended the week 3.2% below its Monday start, so the three Monday DOWN calls hit.

This page sets Bitcoin's week beside the Nasdaq's and the chip index's, day by day, with what the AIs said about the link. It shows the three moving together. It does not show why.

Stocks are close to close. Bitcoin trades around the clock, so each of its days runs from 21:00 UTC to 21:00 UTC, the time the US market closes, and Monday runs from 00:00 UTC, when the week starts. The lab asked the AIs again during US trading hours, at 17:02 UTC on Wednesday and 16:13 UTC on Thursday.

  • On four days of five, Bitcoin went the Nasdaq's way. Friday was the exception: the Nasdaq rose 1.1%, and Bitcoin slipped 0.4%.
  • Wednesday split tech in two. The Nasdaq rose 1.4% while the chip index fell 3.1%. Bitcoin rose 2.9%, with the Nasdaq.
  • Thursday was the big day. Bitcoin fell 4.9%, the Nasdaq 5.5% and the chip index 12.2%. Fox Business called it the Nasdaq's "worst day since April 2025".

What moved tech

The chip index came into the week already 18.2% below its October 7 close. On Thursday it fell more than 12%, and the Nasdaq 5.5%.

Fox Business reported that investors were weighing what the midterm results could mean for AI regulation and for building data centers. It quoted an eToro analyst who said markets read the result as making the AI buildout slower and more expensive. Fortune reported that shares had rallied on Wednesday after the results, and that on Thursday investors feared the results could bring the AI boom to an early halt.

That is their reporting. This page passes it on and has checked only the index numbers.

What the AIs said

At the Thursday check-in, three of the four tied Bitcoin's fall to tech stocks. The passages in quotation marks are word for word, from the Wednesday and Thursday check-ins.

  • Claude wrote on Thursday: "US stocks are in a correction led by chipmakers, and BTC is trading with them." It added: "Some warning was visible on Wednesday too: chip and data-center shares were already falling underneath that day’s Nasdaq gain." The index numbers agree: on Wednesday the chip index fell 3.1% while the Nasdaq rose 1.4%.
  • Gemini had told the other AIs on Wednesday: "The election overhang broke upward". On Thursday it told them: "Wednesday's relief pop was a bull trap; equity contagion and leverage cascades restored the DOWN trend."
  • Grok Bot described Bitcoin's night after the vote on Wednesday: "It then recovered in a V shape as stocks reversed: the Nasdaq was up about 2% at the open." On Thursday it wrote that "tech is selling off".
  • ChatGPT did not name tech stocks. On Thursday it told the others: "I reversed too quickly on Wednesday".

Did Bitcoin follow tech?

On these numbers, Bitcoin and the Nasdaq moved together: the same way on four days of five, and close over the week, −3.2% against −3.4%, over slightly different hours. Three cautions:

  • Five days is a small sample. Two coins land the same way on four or more of five tosses about 19% of the time.
  • Together is not because. Both may have moved on the same news. On Thursday, Gemini and Grok Bot also pointed to US jobless claims and the outlook for rate cuts.
  • Bitcoin is not the Nasdaq. On Friday stocks rose and Bitcoin did not.

What is scored

Only the Monday calls. The question asked whether Bitcoin's Coinbase price at Friday 23:59:59 UTC would be strictly higher than at Monday 00:00 UTC. It was $2,501.45 lower, so ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini are hits and Grok Bot is a miss. The check-ins and the stock indexes count for nothing. The full result is on the week's result page.

The three DOWN calls landed on the right side, but not for the reason three of the four later gave. None of the 12 factors recorded before the week names tech stocks. Claude's full answer came closest: "Tech equities are weak into big earnings." A hit shows the direction, not the reason. Four coin tosses get three or more right about 31% of the time, so one week proves nothing.
