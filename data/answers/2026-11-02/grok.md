**News and flows.**

- The record ~$6.3B Binance hack, which the FBI attributes to North Korea's TraderTraitor group, has weighed on the market since Thursday. Binance says it has covered about 90–95% of the loss and started phased withdrawals on 1 November. If those withdrawals run smoothly, one source of risk goes away. If they don't, it comes back.
- US spot Bitcoin ETFs had net outflows of $643M on 30 October, the biggest daily outflow of the month, according to Farside as cited by [CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/bitcoins-recovery-faces-rising-derivatives-exposure-and-643-million-in-etf-outflows/).
- Bitfinex analysts name $75k as support and $80k as the level BTC needs to reclaim for a recovery.

**Sentiment, derivatives and on-chain data.**

- The Fear & Greed Index ([alternative.me](https://alternative.me/crypto/fear-and-greed-index/)) is 34 (Fear), up from 30 and well down from 70 in early October. Extreme fear has often come before short-term bounces, but Investing.com notes that past drops this sharp were followed by lower prices a month later.
- Binance perpetual funding is +0.01% per 8 hours, which is neutral, so there's no crowded long position to flush out.
- [TokenPost/CryptoQuant](https://www.tokenpost.com/news/investing/45851) show net outflows of about 14.4k BTC from exchanges and falling exchange reserves, which is supportive. They also show SOPR below 1 (coins being spent at a loss) and falling volume and active addresses, which point to a weak bounce.
- Futures open interest is at a five-week high, so liquidations could swing the price sharply either way.

**Macro calendar.** Tuesday 3 November is the US midterm election day, which could bring a swing in risk appetite either way. Friday 6 November brings the US jobs report. It is released at 13:30 UTC, well before the 23:59:59 UTC check, so its reaction will be fully priced in.

**Weighing it.** The case for DOWN is the downtrend, ETF outflows, weak volume and lingering hack risk. The case for UP is fearful sentiment after a sharp drop, neutral funding, coins leaving exchanges and the hack loss being largely covered. Over one week, BTC's direction is close to a coin flip. With the selling showing signs of tiring and the Binance overhang easing, I lean very slightly UP, with low confidence.
