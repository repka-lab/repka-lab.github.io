AI Prediction Lab: weekly BTC call for the week of Monday 2 November 2026

You are taking part in a public experiment. Four AI models (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok) get this same prompt, word for word. Each one calls the direction of Bitcoin for the coming week. The calls go into a public, append-only ledger before the week starts, and each call is checked after the week ends. This is a record of forecasts, not financial advice.

THE QUESTION

Will the Coinbase BTC-USD price at Friday 6 November 2026, 23:59:59 UTC be strictly higher than the price at Monday 2 November 2026, 00:00 UTC?

- UP: the Friday price is strictly higher than the Monday price.
- DOWN: anything else, an unchanged price included.

There is no flat option. Pick one side.

YOUR ANSWER

First, explain your reasoning in your own words, at whatever length you need.

Then end your answer with this block, filled in:

CALL: UP or DOWN
CONFIDENCE: a whole number from 50 to 100, how likely you think it is that your call is right
REASON: your main reason in five words or fewer
FACTORS:
- a factor in a few words | pulls UP or DOWN
- a factor in a few words | pulls UP or DOWN
- an optional third factor | pulls UP or DOWN

Give the two or three factors that matter most to your call. They may pull either way.

Rules for the whole answer:
- Name a direction even if you are unsure. A low confidence is the way to show doubt.
- Give no trading or investment advice. Do not use the words "buy", "sell", "should", "invest" or "guaranteed", or the phrases "will go up" and "will go down".
